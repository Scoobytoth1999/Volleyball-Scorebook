SHORT DESCRIPTION (first lines buyers see)
Keep the official volleyball game book on a laptop or Chromebook. Tap the team that won each rally, and the scorebook tracks the server, rotation, side-outs, subs, and timeouts for you. When the match ends, print or save a finished school-style or club-style scoresheet as a PDF.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Tired of erasing, recounting subs, and arguing about who should be serving? Digital Volleyball Scorebook turns your laptop into the scorer's table.

HOW IT WORKS
• Set up the match: team names, team colors, level, and officials. Pick a level from 4th grade through college and the rules fill themselves in.
• Enter lineups on a court diagram with an on-screen number keypad. Mark captains and liberos.
• Check both rotations before the first serve.
• Tap the team that won the rally. The server, rotation, side-outs, and running score update automatically.
• Print or save the finished scoresheet as a PDF.

FEATURES
• Two scoresheet styles: School (NFHS rally scoring) and Club (USAV/AAU style)
• Live log of the last few rallies, showing who served each point, to settle disputes
• Undo, plus an editable game log to fix a mistake several rallies back
• Counts subs and timeouts. Illegal subs are blocked, with an injury (exceptional) sub override.
• Libero tracking, playing captain, yellow and red cards, penalty points, and replays
• Lineup check view laid out like the court as seen from the scorer's table
• Best-of-3, best-of-5, or play-every-set formats; editable points, timeouts, and sub limits
• "Copy from" earlier set lineups and "Start new match" for back-to-back matches
• Blank printable school and club sheets for a paper backup
• Back up and restore all games with one file
• Works offline. No accounts, no logins, no data sent anywhere.

REQUIREMENTS — PLEASE READ BEFORE BUYING
• Chrome or Edge on a Windows, Mac, or Chromebook computer. Touchscreen laptops work great.
• NOT for iPad or iPhone. iPads can't run a downloaded web file.
• Games are saved in the browser on the computer where you use the file. Use the built-in backup to move games between computers.

GREAT FOR
Middle school and high school volleyball coaches, team managers, parent scorekeepers, athletic directors, club teams, and PE teachers running volleyball units.
